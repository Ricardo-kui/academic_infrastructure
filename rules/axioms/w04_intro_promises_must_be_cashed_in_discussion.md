# w04: Intro Promises Must Be Cashed in Discussion — 跨 Section 对齐是投稿前最低门槛

## 核心原则

**Introduction 承诺了什么，Discussion 就必须兑现什么；兑现不了的承诺是 overclaim，是 desk reject 的直接原因。**

Pollock Ch02: "故事的 knot 必须在 Discussion 被解开。"
Pollock Ch08: "Discussion 不是 result restatement，而是 contribution delivery。"

## 承诺-兑现对照表

| Introduction 承诺 | Discussion 必须兑现 | 常见断裂 |
|------------------|-------------------|---------|
| "本文挑战了假设X" | "我们的发现表明假设X在Z情境下失效" | 结果只支持局部关系，却声称推翻整个理论 |
| "机制是M" | "M 的中介效应被证实/替代机制被排除" | 主模型显著但机制检验缺失 |
| "边界条件是B" | "B 的调节效应符合理论预测" | 调节假设不显著却声称"总体支持" |
| "对A群体有启示" | "A 群体应该改变对Y的理解" | Implications 比结果更宽 |
| "使用D设计解决内生性问题" | "D 设计的结果与主检验一致" | 识别策略和结果叙述脱节 |

## 执行规则

1. **投稿前必须做 promise-cash 审计**：列出 Introduction 的每个 claim，检查 Discussion 是否对应
2. **Discussion 第一段必须回答 Introduction 的最后问题**：形成首尾呼应
3. **禁止 Discussion 引入 Introduction 未承诺的新贡献**：新发现如果重要，必须说明是"exploratory"
4. **Limitations 必须说明哪些承诺因何原因未能完全兑现**：诚实比 overclaim 安全

## 反例 → 正例

- ❌ Introduction: "This study challenges the assumption that institutional investors uniformly pressure managers to minimize liability."
  Discussion: "Our findings contribute to the literature on product recalls by showing that common ownership matters."
- ✅ Introduction: "This study challenges the assumption that institutional investors uniformly pressure managers to minimize liability, by showing that overlapping ownership creates competitive-softening incentives that delay recalls."
  Discussion: "Our finding that common ownership delays recalls challenges the default assumption that institutional investors always push for faster liability minimization. Instead, the effect depends on the competitive structure of the industry: common ownership delays recalls only when rival firms' market positions would be harmed by early disclosure. This boundary condition sharpens the theory by showing that investor pressure is not uniform but contingent on portfolio overlap."
