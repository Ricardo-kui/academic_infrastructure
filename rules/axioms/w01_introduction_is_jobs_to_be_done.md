# w01: Introduction Is Jobs to Be Done — 引言不是文献空白展示

## 核心原则

**引言是面向目标读者的"待完成任务"：说明研究如何为特定受众创造 gain 或缓解 pain。**

Simsek & Li: "A strong scholarly introduction designs and presents a specific utility for a target audience."

Pollock Ch05: "Introduction 不是背景介绍，而是故事的启动装置。"

## 六 Block 结构

| Block | 功能 | 读者问题 |
|-------|------|---------|
| 1. Target audience | 让目标读者可见 | "这跟我有什么关系？" |
| 2. Progress/challenges | 建立共享语境 | "已知什么？争议在哪？" |
| 3. Gain/pain | 证明问题值得解决 | "为什么现在要解决？" |
| 4. Proposed solution | 陈述研究答案 | "本文提供什么？" |
| 5. Credibility | 证明答案可信 | "为什么我应该相信？" |
| 6. Implications | 回报价值给受众 | "知道后我该怎么想/做？" |

## 执行规则

1. **第一段必须建立张力（tension）**：用一个具体事实、悖论或反直觉发现开场，不是"X is important"
2. **禁止 gap-spotting 开场**："Despite growing interest in X, little is known about Y" 是万能模板，也是万能无聊
3. **Gain/pain 必须具体到后果**：不是"现有研究忽视了Z"，而是"因为忽视了Z，理论无法解释A现象，导致B后果"
4. **Solution 必须直接回答 gain/pain**：不能让读者猜本文的发现是什么
5. **Credibility 不超载 methods 细节**：提前交代理论依据和研究设计优势，但不罗列变量清单

## 反例 → 正例

- ❌ "Product recalls are important for firm performance. Despite growing research on recalls, few studies have examined CEO personality. This study examines how CEO narcissism affects recall timing."
- ✅ "In 2014, General Motors recalled 2.6 million vehicles for a faulty ignition switch—a defect its engineers had known about for a decade. The delay cost the company $2.5 billion and 124 lives. Why do some firms wait years to recall dangerous products while others act within days? The upper-echelons literature attributes such delays to governance failures and resource constraints, but it largely treats CEOs as interchangeable decision-makers. We argue this assumption overlooks a critical source of heterogeneity: CEO personality. By importing attention-allocation theory from cognitive psychology, we show that narcissistic CEOs accelerate recalls when media visibility is high—because their self-image depends on public admiration, not because they care more about safety. This finding challenges the default view that personality effects on risk decisions are uniformly negative."
